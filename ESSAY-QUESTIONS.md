## The closing four — Cecil's collected questions (59:53 to the end)

1. What is good in life?
2. What does freedom account for?
3. What are people worth?
4. What are we supposed to be pursuing?

## The daily form (50:42)

5. Not "what do I want to do today," but: what should I do today? What duties can I fulfill for my friends and my family?

## The core question — why one asks sages at all (18:40)

6. What is my duty — and what is the best way to fulfill it?

## The loyalty cluster (42:10–42:27)

7. Who should you be loyal to, and why?
8. How do you build a good family group?
9. Who do you take with you? Who is important to you?
10. Who do you defend, and trust, and support — and who defends, trusts, and supports you?

## The fundamental challenge (47:50–48:06)

11. Why would you sacrifice the partner you said you loved — for money, respect, career, ego?

## The story asking back

12. (22:45) Why would you write out — and kill — those you actually love and care for, because that is your job, your duty rightly understood?
13. (13:05–14:32) The impaled yogi to the king, and then to Dharma himself: why did you run me through? What is wrong with you? How is this just?
14. (33:34) Vritra, mid-war: why are we fighting?
15. (41:15) Karna to his mother: where have you been all these years — you left me out in the cold.
